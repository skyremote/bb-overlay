# Screen Share

Hide names, email addresses, phone numbers and amounts on screen before you share it, record it or demo a chat on a call.

## What you get

- One switch (`/screenshare`, Cmd+Alt+S, the + menu or `bb screen-share on`) that paints a grey bar over personal details across the whole of bb, including thread titles and tabs.
- A strip above the chat box while it is on, with a Turn off button.
- Every window and remote client switches together, so your phone view is covered too.

## How it works

The masking is visual only: it paints over text with the browser's highlight API and never changes the page, so streaming replies, copy and paste and the agent's own view are untouched. Your on or off choice is stored by the plugin on your bb server. Nothing leaves your machine.
