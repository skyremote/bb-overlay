# Reflect

Turn the rules you give your agents into rules they keep. When a message you send contains a lasting rule ("from now on never use hashtags in my posts"), Reflect shows a card above the chat box with the rule tidied up, and saves it with one press to your global CLAUDE.md, your own voice or style guide, or your notes tool.

Detection is a fixed pattern check that runs on your bb server: no AI, no cost, and nothing leaves your machine. Saves only ever add a line, skip rules you already have and back the file up first.
